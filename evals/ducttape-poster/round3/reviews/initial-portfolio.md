# Independent blind audience and portfolio review

I read only `blind-initial/cases.json`, `blind-initial/rubric.md`, and the 16 anonymous images in each of the native, usage, and thumbnail directories. All 48 views were inspected individually. Native files were viewed with original detail; usage files are 720 px wide, and thumbnails have a 320 px long side. PNG dimensions were checked directly. No maker explanations, mappings, prompts, skills, or other reviews informed this assessment. These ratings describe the visible outcome, not its authorship.

The clear audience floor failure is **S5's title hierarchy**. Its only required communication becomes a nearly invisible vertical inscription at thumbnail size. The other consequential weaknesses are smaller: **C1's supporting instructions are cramped at the specified 720 px usage width**, and **P2 does little to explain the folding proposition without reading its product name**. No broad style ban is supported. N1 and N2 visibly meet the expressly requested 3D title, chrome, and strong neon conditions; those effects should stay.

## Scoped failures and supported fixes

1. **S5, definite audience/type floor failure:** enlarge and strengthen the title within its dark corridor until the full three-word title establishes first sight at 320 px. Preserve the botanical passage and detailed blue material. A larger three-word grouping would also remove the vertical word-break ambiguity. Reinspect both 320 px and native type edges; do not sacrifice the best image work merely to simplify the style.
2. **C1, practical-reading floor weakness:** increase the 720 px size of preparation, weather cancellation, and certificate conditions. Recover space from the headline/image or regroup the information; preserve every exact supplied word and the highly visible deadline/URL. The problem is supporting copy size, so making the already successful CTA still larger would not fix it.
3. **S3/S4/T5/P2, high-end specificity limitations:** make the dominant visual decision explain the particular subject. For P2, an independent folding/portability proposition should become visible, not depend entirely on `Fold Cup`. For the garden directions, the flower/type/space relationship should communicate nocturnal opening or the experience/structure of a garden. Painting, cut material, flat type, glow, and studio photography can all remain; none is itself the failure. T5 most needs an intentional flower-to-letter relationship and calmer control of the compressed bottom row.
4. **P1/P2, narrow technical constraint issue:** native dimensions are not exactly 4:5. Normalize to an exact integer 4:5 canvas, such as 1120×1400 with a negligible crop, and recheck type margins. This 0.036% ratio deviation is not a visible craft defect, but it should not be reported as an exact ratio pass.
5. **Portfolio gate:** select alternatives on their actual native finish, 720 px information reading, 320 px first sight, and independent proposition. These examples support that targeted gate. They do not support banning chrome, neon, minimalism, pale paper, texture, or any genre, nor a claim about the success rate of other unseen requests.

No confirmed word omission or obvious spelling corruption was found in this packet. Remaining uncertainty is specifically the raster-only dash identity, botanical species/behavior, physical product capacity/material proof, and S5's strict vertical-break interpretation. These are not converted into invented errors. The strong native finishes in S2, S5, T3, and P1 also should not conceal the separately stated typography, audience, and portfolio limitations.
