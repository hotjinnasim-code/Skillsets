# Coast supplemental blind portfolio comparison

This is a scoped supplement after my completed 17-variant review, **not a fresh four-case blind context**. I inspected only this supplement's permitted README, cases, pairs, rubric, native metadata and individual PNGs. No mappings, prompts, production paths, repository revisions or peer reports were accessed. Pair order supplies no chronology or target outcome.

T8 gives the dense information two meaningful groups and restores collective participation through its panoramic illustration. Its selection is not a claim that every dimension improves over T3. **Content/form and visible slop avoidance remain 3 in both selected posters.** Selected-set diversity: **4**, with a recurring headline/footer habit explicitly retained below.

## Located causes for the scores

**R7**

- **C3:** A glove/picker lifts a crushed bottle above a bag in the upper picture, directly explaining cleanup. The overall photograph/checklist/colored-footer package supplies little editorial invention beyond literal illustration.
- **H4:** Headline and recruitment line lead into a bold date/time at the start of the cream block; the green bottom strip isolates the deadline, complete URL and contact. All detail is readable at width720. At 320 the application strip remains easy to find, but its URL is small.
- **T4:** Korean, numerals and URL are crisp, with consistent left alignment and useful headline/date/body size changes. The eight-line supporting block remains one undifferentiated list rather than several semantic groups.
- **S4:** The hand cropped from the upper-right and diagonal picker balance the open sky and large bag; the lower text preserves a stable left margin. Hard horizontal boundaries keep the picture, conditions and action separate.
- **F4:** Knit glove, metal tool, dirt-coated bottle and translucent bag folds are convincing at native size; visible contact and light are coherent. No conspicuous extra finger or broken tool edge was observed.
- **E4:** Enlarging the physical pickup makes participation purposeful rather than merely decorative; sea and shore remain secondary.
- **A3:** No obvious synthetic defect is needed for this score. Topic-matched stock-style action above a plain checklist and solid CTA band is a familiar package; type and image do not establish a distinctive larger editorial argument.

**R2**

- **C3:** Two participants and a shared bag express `함께`, but the same three-band architecture remains conventional.
- **H4:** Same effective headline/date/time/green-action-strip sequence as R7; full copy reads at 720. Faces and clothing compete with the smaller picker/bottle action at 320.
- **T4:** Clean Korean/Latin/numerals, appropriate supporting size steps and orderly left-aligned groups. The information list has the same limited semantic subdivision.
- **S4:** Bent standing figure on the left and crouching figure on the right form a balanced triangle around the bag, with a clear lower checklist field.
- **F4:** Clothing, gloves, shore, picker and bag have coherent photographic finish and grounded contact; no conspicuous structural failure was observed.
- **E3:** The participation message functions, though the familiar posed-looking recruitment tableau is less focused than R7's close action.
- **A3:** The people-and-beach image plus checklist/footer could serve many volunteer appeals; correct text and coherent rendering do not by themselves resolve that generic editorial habit.

**T8**

- **C3:** Left participant uses a picker; right participant holds a bag and collects litter. The panorama communicates collective cleanup, but header, information and picture remain recognizably a conventional recruitment flyer rather than a singular graphic concept.
- **H4:** Centered headline/recruitment line lead to two groups: date/time/place/eligibility on the left, provisions/preparation/rain/fee policy on the right. The rust bottom band makes deadline and complete URL conspicuous. All details read at width720; supporting text is smaller than T3's.
- **T4:** Clean heavy headline, lighter body and readable white application type. Body columns preserve word boundaries and useful spacing; centering the headline and footer around left-aligned columns is coherent but familiar.
- **S4:** Wide shoreline joins the two lower figures, while the upper columns use the landscape width well. The narrow scenic band compresses the physical action, and the large footer claims substantial height.
- **F4:** Human forms, hats, glove/tool contacts, bag, beach and waves share a clean illustration system. Simplified faces are stylistic; no evident malformed anatomy or incoherent material transition was observed.
- **E4:** Clothing, coordinated action and open shore make recruitment approachable. Neither illustration nor restrained color is automatically a strength; these elements support the shown activity.
- **A3:** Familiar friendly volunteer figures, scenic beach, centered heavy heading and solid footer form an interchangeable package. Better information grouping improves use, without removing the generic artistic treatment.

**T3**

- **C4:** The curved shoreline defines the edge between the cream information field and the pickup picture, connecting information structure to coast geometry.
- **H4:** Top headline/recruitment line, continuous left checklist, then yellow application strip. Deadline and URL are especially quick to find at 320; all detail reads at 720.
- **T4:** Strong navy Korean and mixed numerals/URL, consistent left alignment and clear size hierarchy. Eight body lines remain one dense sequence, with less semantic separation than T8.
- **S4:** The right-column hand, diagonal picker and bag occupy a coherent shoreline field; longest body lines stay clear of its edge. Title and solid footer retain conventional heavy-band framing.
- **F4:** Transparent bottle, bag facets, glove and picker are consistently illustrated. Wave and shore edges are clean, with plausible visible object relationships.
- **E4:** Close action and strong beach color make the subject direct and active; yellow emphasizes application rather than unrelated decoration.
- **A4:** Content-specific boundary and clearly depicted pickup give more specificity than a generic photograph/list split. The isolated hand/picker/bottle scene and heavy-footer habit still limit exceptional originality.

## Selected-set portfolio assessment

**R7 + T8 diversity: 4/5.** R7 uses an upper photographic close action and sky headline, followed by one full-width lower checklist. T8 places a centered headline and two information groups above a wide collective beach illustration. Image purpose, image/type ordering, text grouping, alignment and narrative distance differ beyond a color or surface swap. The shared enormous sans recruitment heading and full-width colored application footer remain a strong structural habit, preventing 5.

**Best/weakest kept separate:** T3 has the most consistent editorial specificity among these four, particularly the shoreline information boundary, but T8 offers the selected landscape's stronger grouping and collective narrative. Within the chosen set, R7's pickup rendering and T8's information grouping are the strongest practical properties. **Both selected posters' weakest core dimensions remain C3 and A3.** Diversity4 does not repair those scores, and no averaging or strongest-image compensation is used. This supplement does not certify an entire four-case portfolio or future outputs.
