Its T4/M4/A4 are not upgraded to 5 simply because the arrangement is appealing: the brush-like lettering is still conventional, and fine foliage remains generalized. **Weakest single craft candidate: B2**, relative to this batch. It is functional and visibly faithful, with no proved glyph or botanical error, but the upper title and repeated floral/stamen modules below remain a generic botanical poster rather than a tightly specific editorial construction. Its A3 stays explicit. V2, J1 and L1 likewise retain A3 for the located generic packages described above, not for authorship or their genre.

**Selected below-4 core finding: M1 A3.** Its readable complete copy, correct ratio and H5 do not cancel that weakness. The coast set's diversity is also only 3. Accordingly the full selected batch does **not** uniformly show the rubric's 4–5 core and diversity signal for a high-end candidate; the selected coast result is the clear retained limitation. No mean, total, or preference in another dimension is used to hide it.

Visible-copy checks found no confirmed literal error. Remaining uncertainty concerns underlying encoding, real capacity/material/mechanism, illustrative botanical anatomy and V2's virtual title support. These are limits of what the images can establish, not invented visible-copy failures or a human-origin verdict.
