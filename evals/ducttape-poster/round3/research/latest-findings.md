## Most useful additions beyond the existing candidate A guidance

1. **Specify an expressive mark rule.** When handwriting or rough illustration is requested, define what stays consistent across line direction, stroke ends, pressure/edge changes and simplification. Inspect whether irregularity is deliberate and readable; do not assume smoother is better. A selected rule can be clean or rough.
2. **Connect the motion of type and illustration.** A shared slant, curve behavior or contour energy can integrate mixed elements more concretely than matching their grain or color. This is one possible relation, not a new mandatory package.
3. **Give practical information a designed reading zone.** For dense content, arrange the real semantic groups and keep the chosen motif from randomly fragmenting them. Avoid treating all secondary information as identical footer copy; preserve every user-supplied item.
4. **Allow ordinary specificity.** A real everyday object, unidealized gesture, vernacular font or observational moment can be the memorable choice. Ask what is characteristic about it and construct the image around that, rather than adding exotic materials to signal sophistication.

These are application inferences from actual recent work, not a style ranking proven by statistics. The visible quality target can be tested on actual outputs; absolute absence of AI slop across arbitrary future requests cannot be guaranteed by sources or instructions alone.
