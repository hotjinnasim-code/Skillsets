## Five actionable improvements

1. **Start with a visible fact or relationship, and rank habitual packages below it.** Derive the focal form from the subject's action, object, material, environment or emotional tension. A tap shoe can be a strong direct idea; subject specificity need not mean a complicated metaphor. Where a style axis is unspecified, ask what in the actual subject justifies that selection. Respect an explicit neon, chrome, collage, paper or other style. Rejecting one habitual package must not install cream paper/editorial serif as a replacement default. This is an application inference from the Public Theater and frontend-design sources below.

2. **Research the current problem, then transfer a construction principle.** Choose a few actual references that match a relevant constraint such as dense Korean copy, a photographic focal subject, or an illustration-led emotional message. Record how the title, subject and information occupy space, and what production rule makes the result coherent. Translate that rule into the current content rather than importing the source's copy, logo, famous composition or all of its aesthetics. Compare the actual references and generated result at the same displayed size. The Sinfonietta series demonstrates both graphic variety and subject response; this workflow is an inference, not a universal studio rule.

3. **Give the title a concrete typographic construction and the information its own role.** Specify width/weight, phrase grouping, line rhythm, shape of the title block, and its exact contact with the focal image. Check Hangul's internal stroke spaces and between-syllable spaces, then optical alignment and mixed-script density. A coherent typographic rule may be condensed, hand-drawn, flat, distorted or rhythmic; it need not be 3D, restrained, or distorted in every direction. The title's expressive integration must preserve essential reading. This extends the skill's existing glyph checks into an explicit construction decision. Primary evidence: Public Theater and Professionalism; optical/glyph application is inferred.

4. **Art-direct photographs as photographs, not an adjective package.** Decide viewpoint and camera distance, crop, focal plane, light direction/softness, the principal shadow and each material's response. State what the subject is doing or what the photographed objects reveal. Verify structure, contact, depth and coherent lighting in the actual result. Avoid solving every photo with generic cinematic rim light, shallow depth, smooth skin or glossy surfaces; retain those when the brief justifies them. Version Industries explicitly describes prop selection, scene building, lighting and photography. The concrete production and QA checks are our inference from that process and the viewed images.

5. **Art-direct illustrations through form and mark-making.** Decide silhouette/gesture, how lines begin/end, which edges stay crisp or disappear, how flat and modelled areas relate, and which details earn attention. Put texture where the chosen drawing/printing method would create it, rather than overlaying identical grain on subject, lettering and background. Check the important gesture/object remains recognizable at thumbnail size. A warm hand-drawn request can yield many distinct constructions within the requested style. Ida Henrich directly ties Tár's metronome and moving hands to the film's theme of control; the line/edge/detail instructions are inferred from the actual illustration.

## Limits and evaluation implications

These are observed design principles, not evidence that particular effects are statistically common in AI output, nor a method for detecting human or AI authorship. Studio claims of awards are not a generated poster quality test. The prior evaluation was concentrated in exhibition, sparse monochrome and warm repair-workshop requests. A broad next evaluation should include photographic consumer/event work, lively illustration, long practical information, and an explicitly requested strong digital effect. Judge both subject fit and medium execution; do not reward paper grain or clean minimalism by themselves.
