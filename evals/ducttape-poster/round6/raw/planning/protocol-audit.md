# R6 protocol audit — frozen G, twenty fresh originals

Read-only planning audit, 2026-10-08. No image tool calls or live skill edits were made. Sources read: the six live `Skillsets/skills/ducttape-poster` files, `Skillsets/evals/ducttape-poster/round5/report.md`, the R5 G phase plan, review template, cases, maker instructions, manifest example, pre-archive audit, and closed F/G call record. This note adopts the root's chosen ordinary-use protocol below.

## What this round can establish

The six live files match R5's frozen G hashes exactly. R5's PASS is a selected cross-version 14-image bundle plus a required one-image control; G itself had only three fresh originals. R6 can supply the missing evidence of **four complete, newly generated five-image sets under the same G**. It is a new user-authorized phase, not a reopened R5 correction loop. R5 closed at 128 actual calls, with its unused allowance retired.

Freeze all six files and four source requests before the first R6 call. Do not change the skill, seven anchors, requests, or thresholds in response to outputs. R6 permits twenty scheduled initial calls and at most two further native attempts per role, including regeneration, tool-error retries, and root-requested corrections: **sixty native attempts maximum**, twenty final role selections maximum. Count a failed native attempt even if it returns no image; recovering already returned bytes without generating again consumes no native call. Disclose missing initial/final outputs and retain the scheduled-role denominator. Do not repeatedly call until twenty successful first passes can be displayed.

Close at completion of the bounded ordinary production and review, including a truthful final FAIL if defects remain. No critic-guided correction after selection closes, replacement maker used to reset a role's history, new original disguised as a repair, third own correction, or repeat reviews until PASS. No all-future-input, stable success-rate, human-authorship, physical-function, maximum-resolution, or print-readiness claim follows from this sample.

## Production and review order

1. Coordinator derives five distinct directions per request using the frozen skill. Maker packets contain the original request, exact copy/conditions, actual supplied references, short assigned relation and nearest-direction distinction, six frozen files, and evidence-format instructions. They exclude R5 diagnoses, desired scores, exemplars of a “passing” output, critic reports, and other makers' outputs/prompts. Five logical maker roles per case execute in waves of at most three workers while root coordinates. Record observed agent concurrency and native-call overlap separately; capacity is not evidence of overlap.
2. Each maker makes one fresh native call without previous R5/R6 output references. Save the exact call input before calling, preserve the returned native bytes and source SHA, then actually view and manually inspect native, use-size, and thumbnail views. Preserve that initial image and inspection before any self correction. This checkpoint delays repair only for evidence capture; it does not replace ordinary skill judgment with an external test diagnosis.
3. Makers complete normal self inspection and up to two own corrections. Root independently performs the skill's ordinary full-set inspection, including diversity and the weakest image, and can request only the role's remaining shared allowance. Maker and root calls count together. Compare each correction with its actual parent, inspect preservation/regression, and choose the most suitable available stage rather than automatically the latest. Preserve every stage, including rejected corrections and failed attempts. No external critic reports are available to production or selection.
4. Close selections and finalize producer records before freezing review packets. Use fresh reviewer contexts, three independent critics for each **initial five-image case**. If the final physical case differs in any native image/member, use three further fresh critics for its complete final five-image set. If all five final native hashes and set membership match the initial case exactly, bind the entire initial three reviews to that final case and explicitly state that no second review occurred. Do not reuse diversity or individual scores across a changed set.
5. Critics see anonymous images and exact source request/copy/conditions; they do not see phase, creator, direction names, prompts, skill version, maker explanation, selection history, target scores, or peer reviews. Native/use/thumbnail paths must be actually viewed and recorded. Each critic applies all seven unchanged anchors to every image with located evidence, mandatory-copy/condition observations, residuals and uncertainty, plus one full-set diversity/closest-pair judgment. Three separate scores are retained; root does not negotiate them into consensus.

This produces twelve initial case-review assignments and zero to twelve additional final assignments, executed in three-worker batches. Independence is separation of contexts and evidence, not concurrent execution. A critic who fails a viewing or ordering requirement is recorded as a procedural failure. Permit at most one additional remedial critic per case-stage packet for documented procedural invalidity, retaining the invalid report and every score. This bounds the worst case at thirty-two critic assignments. Never replace an adverse valid reviewer. Missing three valid reviews after that allowance means INCOMPLETE, not PASS.

For a dense action request, have the critic first read its anonymous use-size images and commit observed event date, application deadline, complete URL and place, with uncertainty, before opening the exact-copy packet. This tests discoverability without priming the answer. Keep the first reading unchanged, then perform full transcription. Do not apply invented action fields to title-only/product requests. Reviewers may know the kind of task and intended display size, but not the correct field values before this first reading.

## Coverage and avoiding a custom success recipe

Use four **new content requests**, not R5's garden/cup/workshop/roll-night scenes with renamed titles. A practical matrix is:

| Case | Natural user constraint | Main coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Title only; no stated quantity/style/ratio | Default five, default 2:3, no invented copy, sparse typography, five-way distinction |
| B | Fictional product, photographic treatment, mixed Korean/Latin/numerical copy, 4:5 | Material/structure visibility, mixed glyph widths, date/unit/URL fidelity, five photographic alternatives |
| C | Dense event/participation copy with actual action clauses; styles delegated; specified five-image ratio allocation | Longest/smallest copy at use size, deadline/address discoverability, semantic grouping, recomposition without multiplying quantity |
| D | Explicit strong shared illustrative/effect style, preferably with an identifiable action; square or landscape | Narrow-style compliance, illustration/form/contact, effect fidelity, five alternatives inside a common style |

The requests may define real content necessities: a product function, action, exact copy, requested medium, or exclusions. They should not prescribe a layout solution, percentage of image area, fixed reading path, unusual glyph treatment, hand lettering, physical overlap, mandated metaphor, or five predetermined genres. In case B, fictional capacity is copy, not a pixel measurement; do not add unsupported real-brand likeness. In case D, familiar fonts, neon/3D, strong colour or rough texture are valid when coherently resolved. Do not turn their presence/absence into a novelty or genre gate.

Use 720-pixel width as a declared **screen test** only when the source request defines that intended use, or label it the evaluation viewing size rather than an unstated universal skill requirement. Check all supplied copy there, while native size checks glyphs/finish and 320-long-side checks first glance. Preserve aspect when making QA reductions. A predeclared relative ratio tolerance such as R5's 1% belongs to the measurement procedure; report actual dimensions/error and do not call an approximately matching output mathematically exact. Never crop, redraw or composite text onto native outputs to manufacture a pass, and never resize them and count that as native resolution.

Twenty images from four requests provide four context replications, not twenty independent request samples. All four use five roles, so custom totals other than five, one-image follow-up edits, truly text-free posters, missing-copy questions, conflicting inputs, real-logo/screen fidelity, user reference transfer, mixed-ratio totals other than five, exact preservation after a date edit, and collaboration-tool absence remain untested. They are explicit exclusions; do not quietly extend R6 to cover them. A text-only/black-white control is optional coverage only if one of the four original requests genuinely asks for it, not a late extra output required for PASS.

## Scoring and conclusions

Keep `content_form`, `hierarchy`, `typography`, `space`, `image_material`, `emotion_control`, `ai_slop` and their 1/3/5 anchors unchanged. Distinct alternatives do not require campaign uniformity. Familiar/direct presentation can score highly; unfamiliarity alone earns nothing. AI-slop scoring concerns visible finish and editorial choices, not identification of the maker.

For continuity with the declared R5 criterion, every selected image must pass mandatory requirements and each critic's `content_form`, `typography`, `image_material`, `ai_slop` must be at least 4; each complete set's diversity must be at least 4. Retain the other three dimensions, all scores below 4, dissent, and uncertainty. If a stricter all-seven threshold is desired, declare it before calls as an additional R6 standard and report it separately; do not relabel it the unchanged R5 gate.

Report a row per case for first-pass and selected-final gates, weakest scores, failed mandatory checks, repairs used, changed/reused final review, and residuals. Provide all twenty image-level records and all reviewer scores alongside that table. Report initial-to-final improvements and regressions, and do not attribute repair-assisted final quality to initial reliability. A selected previous stage is permitted within the same frozen G role; mixing R5 images or later versions is not fresh G validation. The honest conclusion is “these four fresh five-image G cases passed/failed the predeclared checks,” with the actual first-pass and final results, not an unqualified statement that the skill always produces high-end work.

## Minimal trace, closure and archive

Five small record types are sufficient; no new evaluation framework is needed:

| Record | Required fields |
| --- | --- |
| `phase-plan.json` | Creation UTC; user-authorized new-R6 scope; six-file relative paths/SHA; four requests/copy/conditions; twenty role IDs; gates/view sizes; maximum 60; per-role maximum 3 attempts; reviewer policy; old R5 closed count/reference |
| `production/ROLE/STAGE/manifest.json` | Role/case/stage/attempt index; exact pre-call input file/SHA; actual start/end/status/error; tool-return metadata; returned source path/SHA; preserved original path/SHA/dimensions/ratio; parent path/SHA for edit; actual viewed paths; inspection path/SHA |
| `selection.json` | All twenty required roles; selected stage/native SHA; all attempt records; maker/root corrections combined; rationale/residuals; producer manifest pins; full initial/final case membership hashes; closed selection UTC |
| `reviews/packet/reviewer.json` | Packet SHA and full member/native/QA pins; anonymous IDs; actual viewed paths; action-first record where applicable; required/observed/equal transcriptions; conditions; seven integer scores/located evidence; full-set diversity/nearest pair; residuals; procedural status |
| `closure.json` / `archive-index.json` | Every attempt and actual total; <=60 and <=3/role checks; actual generated/final counts; all required cases/reviews; initial/final outcomes; review reuse binding; failures; unused allowance retired; retained-file relative paths/SHA and archive verification |

Save immutable stage-specific originals, prompts/call inputs, inspections and manifests; do not overwrite them while “finalizing.” Keep a separate selected-final link/copy. Freeze final producer manifests before packet construction to avoid R5's H02 manifest-drift failure; any later corrected metadata creates a new dated record and new packet pin, retaining the failed binding.

Archive the six-file snapshot, requests/plan, all R6 initial and repair native outputs (including unselected ones), source-transfer hashes, QA views actually used, all attempts/errors, selections, anonymous mappings, packets, raw reviews/action-first records, closure and concise report. Preserve the R5 report/closure reference and hash; do not duplicate the whole historical archive or imply omitted old comparison originals are included. The R6 archive should resolve every R6 review image and comparison parent without the old work folder. A lightweight verifier should reject a missing role/case/critic, changed pinned byte, changed set under reused diversity, over-budget attempt, missing native output, and a known score-3/mandatory-failure fixture; do not build tests that merely echo the assembler. Integrity PASS and visual-quality PASS are separate results. Hashes verify bytes, while viewing/order/located visual evidence remain reviewer attestations.

Final closure retires unused R6 attempts even on FAIL. It does not create a reserve for a later unannounced continuation.
