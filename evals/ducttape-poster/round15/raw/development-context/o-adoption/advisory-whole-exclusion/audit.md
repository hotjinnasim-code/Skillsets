# O-draft developmental audit — whole-composition exclusion

## Scope and recommendation

O-draft clearly states the intended exclusion, including a photograph or rendered room used mainly to supply blank planes for the familiar title/date/information arrangement. Its definition concerns the whole image–space–type relation: image coverage, realistic light, surface finish, a serif substitution, and studio reputation cannot establish that the relation changed. It also explicitly protects gothic support/title, useful information groups, negative space, photographic posters, room scenes, and typography-led work when their whole relation differs.

Adopt that definition prospectively, with a small correction to the final-selection/delivery instructions. `SKILL.md` forbids excluded results from becoming finished options, but its delivery section still says to deliver all five; `parallel-production.md` also unconditionally requests a selected final path and delivery of the requested quantity. Those instructions can turn an exhausted, excluded attempt into a nominal finished alternative. The strongest reusable amendment is to make a role's final selection explicitly empty when no eligible attempt exists, retain its attempts, and keep the role in the unresolved requested-role denominator. Two smaller clarifications would make maker source observation and the independence boundary more auditable without expanding the workflow.

This is a NONQUALITY developmental advisory. I know the human preference, case, and candidate; this is not blind. I assign no numerical quality scores or replacement verdicts, and do not replace or supplement the original critics. The pinned case identifies the R14 runtime candidate as N; the freeze describes O-draft as prospective and non-adopted. The selected R14 images therefore illustrate the problem and its boundaries, rather than demonstrate O-draft's runtime effectiveness.

## Audit of the candidate instructions

**Exclusion and exceptions are substantively clear.** `art-direction.md` expressly includes near-solid and split fields, font-only substitutions, full-image backgrounds and empty rooms. `SKILL.md` propagates the definition through planning, native specifications, actual inspection and selection. The evaluation reference keeps this human constraint separate from quality judgments and preserves earlier records. The same material protects negative space and information groups, permits gothic, avoids a photographic/serif/vintage fallback, and says a scene need not be filled with an object when its structure is already sufficient.

The exception is also appropriately scoped: an explicit current request for the whole composition overrides the default in that scope, without another approval ritual. “Minimal,” “modern,” a gothic font request, a photograph request or a type-only request is not automatically that override. Type-only waives the nonglyph requirement only; an alternative type/space relation remains possible. There is no need to require depiction, human gestures, causal transformations or a unique metaphor in every direction. The examples of events, editing and rhythm should be read as alternative evidence of organization, not cumulative obligations.

**Maker observation is required, but the evidence handoff could be tighter.** `visual-research.md` says the maker actually views the original before developing the direction; `parallel-production.md` supplies its path and study role. This correctly goes beyond a central summary or famous studio name. However, the maker return fields do not expressly include what source the maker actually saw or what visible relationship changed a design decision. A central viewing record, a source path in a packet, or pixels attached to a generation call does not establish that the maker semantically observed them. The pinned inputs do not contain maker source-view events, so actual R14 compliance cannot be established here.

**Peer production independence is well protected, with a wording boundary to clarify.** Makers may share the human request, legitimate user assets and relevant study sources, while receiving only their own direction; other makers' plans, images and generation prompts are withheld. Central design and set comparison are intentional, so this is independence of execution and observation, not a claim that concepts were conceived without central coordination. There is a small tension between withholding other plans and handing each maker its distinction from the nearest direction. An abstract contrast can satisfy the latter without exposing a peer's full plan or prompt. The evaluation reference separately protects critic independence; this advisory has no access to their records and does not infer their findings.

**The bounded loop is explicit; final closure has a cross-file conflict.** The initial call plus at most two corrections includes regeneration, failed attempts and central calls. Earlier eligible results may be retained; latest-file selection is not automatic; repeated execution or renaming cannot reset the cap. `SKILL.md` explicitly excludes a remaining violation from finished options and retains it as an unresolved role. In contrast, its later “기본 제작은 다섯 장을 모두 전달한다” and the parallel reference's unconditional selected-final path / “요청 수량을 모두 전달한다” can be read as requiring a final image from every role anyway. The generic delivery path should say what to do when no eligible selection exists. Evaluation must still retain the entire requested-role universe and original records, even though a rejected image is absent from finished delivery.

## Concrete adoption suggestions

### Adopt: make final selection and delivery fail closed

Use the existing exclusion paragraph as the authority and replace the unconditional delivery wording in `SKILL.md` §5 and `parallel-production.md`'s closing/return instructions with the following shared rule. No quality gate or original verdict needs to change:

> FINAL SELECTION은 각 요청 역할마다 현재 적용되는 필수 조건과 기본 스타일 제외를 충족한 시도의 경로를 선택하거나, 적합한 시도가 없으면 NONE과 미해결 사유를 기록한다. 최초 1회와 중앙·제작자 합산 보정 최대 2회의 한도는 유지한다. 제외된 시도와 실패·미선택 이력은 보존하고, 최종 선택이 NONE인 역할도 요청 역할 수와 미해결 분모에 남긴다. 적합한 선택본만 완성 시안으로 전달하며, 미해결 역할은 별도로 알린다. 제외본을 완성 대안으로 재명명하거나 보정본을 새 역할로 세어 수량을 채우지 않는다.

The maker return should allow a selected path **or NONE**; central verification should apply the same current conditions before adopting a returned path. A rejected latest attempt need not invalidate an earlier eligible attempt. If no eligible attempt remains, retaining an excluded image in history does not make it a final option. This does not authorize an extra native attempt, a replacement role or a new approval process.

For evaluation packets, keep an unresolved role identifiable with its available excluded/diagnostic pixels and status as appropriate to the already fixed protocol. Do not delete it from the set, fabricate a final file, or replace an original critic's assessment. Preserve declared FAIL/NA distinctions and records rather than assigning new verdicts through this advisory.

### Adopt: record maker observation in the existing handoff

Add one sentence to the maker return instructions in `parallel-production.md`, cross-referencing the existing research requirement:

> 최초 생성 전에 실제로 본 자기 방향의 조사 이미지 경로와 관찰한 전체 관계, 현재 설계에서 그 관계가 바꾼 결정, 가져오지 않을 요소를 기존 제작 기록에 짧게 남긴다. 원본을 보지 못했다면 중앙 요약이나 파일명으로 실제 관찰을 대신하지 말고 제한을 기록하고 열람 가능한 관련 자료로 보완한다.

This records an existing obligation rather than adding a source-count quota, a new actor, or a requirement to feed study pixels into the native model. Keep three roles distinct: study source for maker observation; user asset with the user's specified preservation/reproduction role; native reference required by a particular editing or preservation call. A local repair requires the exact target image. A study source does not automatically become a required native-image reference, and required user/edit references cannot be waived merely because the maker studied a similar image.

### Adopt: clarify the nearest-direction contrast without sharing peer work

Add a short qualification to the central maker packet instructions:

> 가까운 방향과의 구분은 중앙이 작성한 큰 형태·글자 역할·공간·읽는 흐름의 짧은 대비 조건으로만 전달하며, 다른 제작자의 전체 계획·프롬프트·이미지는 전달하지 않는다.

This reconciles the two current instructions while preserving useful differentiation. Common study sources are permissible when relevant; there is no requirement for five unique sources or a permanent five-style roster.

## Optional cautions, separate from adoption changes

- Keep the existing lists of scene events, distance, crop, editing, drawing and rhythm as examples of possible organizing relations. Do not implement them as required depicted actions, mandatory transformations or a ban on quiet architecture. M3/M5 demonstrate a meaningful scene relationship for this case, not a universal “add people” remedy.
- Keep the whole-relation definition centralized in `art-direction.md` and link it from the other documents. Additional paraphrases should not gradually become a ban on all serif fonts, gothic titles, photography, type-led work, rooms, panels or solid negative space.
- Treat the five displayed study sources as illustrative observations only. The current `visual-research.md` explicitly disclaims a fixed style list. Neither those sources nor this audit justify area/font/count allocations, a photographic-only fallback, a subdued vintage default or copying source content/composition.
- The pinned freeze lists hashes and identifies scope, but those declarations are not evidence of tool observation, maker independence or completed selection. No hash or runtime verification was performed in this advisory.

## Limitations

Only the exact selected native files were examined. I did not inspect earlier or unselected attempts, maker prompts, source-view logs, call histories or selection records; stage names do not prove the total call count or that excluded attempts remained in history. The fixed cap and retention behavior are audited as instructions, not certified as executed.

All image observations used the original-detail displays. No separate exact usage720 or thumbnail assets were permitted/read, so this report does not certify practical-size copy fidelity, punctuation, information retrieval, print suitability or the original quality gates. The image table discusses whole visual relationships and developmental risks, without issuing replacement poster verdicts.

The source pages were not opened, and no unseen case was run. O-draft's generic wording can be adopted with the proposed closure clarification, but these known development outputs cannot certify universal future quality, successful transfer or a future absence of the rejected pattern.
