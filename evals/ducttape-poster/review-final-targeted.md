## What improved

The new paper has credible fibrous detail, edge thickness, and a shadow that follows its curved separation from the wall. The orange surface has a plausible different scale and texture. This is more materially convincing than the old version's even blue grain over flat geometric shapes. The curve also makes a clear large-scale division between title and lower information. Black text stays readable on both fields, and the title remains identifiable at thumbnail size. The new poster has a calmer viewing pace that suits the act of attending to a small sound.

## What regressed or remains weak

The old version's building-like slabs, narrow channels, and repeated slots provided at least a competent urban/rhythmic reading. Its limitation was specificity: the bars resembled generic vents or equalizer marks, and the large heavy sans headline plus saturated textured planes was a familiar music-poster treatment. Those defects remain real; the old image is not exceptional.

The new version removes even that partial acoustic/urban structure. A single peel edge is visually fluent, but the title does not interact with it in a way that clarifies the sound theme. Unlike poster 01's title dividing across the actual joint, the new title occupies a safe flat field above the curve. The date is isolated at the top and the remaining logistics sit below the edge; these decisions are readable, but the lettering does not turn the material into a more particular listening proposition.

The new image also uses a familiar macro-material composition: large off-white torn paper, textured colored backing, clean black type. Its execution is coherent, with no salient synthetic corruption, but it could support many unrelated craft, publishing, or design events. Better surface realism therefore does not raise its anti-slop score above competent. The old version was generic in a different way. The new pair remains sufficiently diverse as independent alternatives, yet now both rely on close material surfaces and literal edges rather than offering the old pair's photographic/graphic contrast.

## Keep decision and confidence

**Keep the old poster 02.** Its remaining defects are generic rhythm bars, broadly familiar bold music-poster typography, and uniformly applied grain. They do not erase its clearer connection to urban passages and rhythm. The new poster's superior craft cannot compensate for weakening the requested subject, which is a central constraint rather than a decorative preference. Poster 01 already supplies the more convincing quiet material/gap interpretation.

Confidence is moderate. Viewers can read the new paper peel as an urban gap, so I do not claim that interpretation is impossible. The judgment is that its sound-specific visualization is too underdeveloped to justify retaining it over the old candidate. No further generation or file replacement was performed as part of this review.
