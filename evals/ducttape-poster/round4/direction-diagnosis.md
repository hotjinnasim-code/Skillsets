## What remains generic, and why

### Coast

The current portrait has three almost perfectly separable rectangles: beach photograph and large two-line heading above, a cream information field below, then a teal application band. The tool–bottle–bag contact in the original is unusually clear and deserves preservation as an image strength. However, none of the information following the picture affects that picture's scale, boundary or internal organization. The eight operational lines are a single rhythm. The rain notification, free participation and absence of a volunteer certificate therefore receive nearly the same visual treatment as the provided equipment. The URL is easy to recover, but its placement is a reusable footer destination.

The portrait repair introduces two cooperative roles, yet preserves the headline location, image boundary, date block, sentence stack and application band almost exactly. At usage size, the larger people also reduce the picker/bottle junction's visual size. This is a trade from close action to collective activity inside the same composition. More people do not solve the structural issue.

The original landscape has a useful coast-shaped boundary between the left information field and the right pickup action. That is a real content/form relationship. It is weakened by the very large headline crossing toward the illustration, the similar heavy weights throughout the copy, and the full-width yellow footer. The composition still resolves into headline, list, side vignette and action band. The landscape repair groups attendance separately from preparation/policy, but places a small beach panorama beneath those groups. At width 720 its body is roughly 16 px, while the heading and application line remain large. The panoramic strip supplies friendliness more than a new reason for the information's shape.

The raw reports disagree on whether the coast-shaped boundary and close-action crop merit 3 or 4 in specificity/slop avoidance; they consistently identify the repeated heading/list/footer habit. Their selection disagreements also preserve a genuine conflict: stronger semantic grouping versus larger, more continuous practical copy. A redesign must secure both, rather than pay for grouping by shrinking the longest sentences.

### Product

The original P01 immediately identifies a cup and its two states. Its weaknesses are spatial and typographic: `FOLN`, `Fold Cup` and the Korean claim occupy three large stacked lines before the objects; release/order details form another stack underneath. The useful state comparison is treated like an interchangeable packshot between advertising headers and footers. The unused height above the folded object is visually incidental, even though that lost height is the product's principal feature.

The P01 repair convincingly separates linen, wood, green body and translucent lid. The lid transmitting the weave is a real material strength. But the top-down open collapsed body reads as a shallow bowl, and nothing in that image demonstrates re-expansion. The cloth diagonal organizes the composition more strongly than the folding structure does. The serif title and split footer are competent, yet they can accompany many domestic objects. Better materials were purchased with weaker cup/function recognition. This explains why the craft/portfolio reviewers preferred L2 while the design reviewer preferred L1 and retained content/form and slop-avoidance 3s for L2.

P02 is the stronger functional premise: hand pressure, partly compressed bands and a folded comparison explain the claim. Its generic residue comes from the dominant upper-right name, large lower claim, and stacked launch information. The arm makes a useful diagonal, but most type only occupies available empty areas. Preserve the clear physical contact, and give the folding state a deliberate relationship to the type rather than merely moving the title to another corner.

The remedy is not a ban on linen, studio photography, serif type, illustration, figures or colored application fields. It is also not a requirement for custom lettering. A straightforward functional poster can be the strongest answer if its scale, grouping, boundaries and photograph are specific and precise. Each final product image must itself explain the cup/function; another image in the pair cannot do that work for it.
