# Blind finished-image review, round 2

This review uses only `brief.md` and C1, C2, D1, and D2 in `blind-round2`. I inspected each original at 1024 × 1536 and each at the same 360 × 540 thumbnail size. I did not consult maker explanations, prompts, other reviews, skill versions, or a version mapping. The event details are fictional supplied copy.

Scope clarification: each pair is two independently finished alternative styles. A shared campaign identity, consistent font system, or visual family is not a requirement. This corrected review removes family coherence from the comparative judgment. The initial report is preserved separately as `review-design-round2-initial.md`.

## Non-compensable requirements

**No visible blocker in either pair.** Each set contains two images, and every image is exactly 2:3 portrait. No person or instrument photograph appears. Each image visibly contains the supplied title, subtitle, date, opening-hours/closure line, venue, address, admission line, and organizer line, with no added copy. I found no visibly malformed Hangul or altered event detail. This is a visual fidelity assessment; a raster image does not establish the underlying Unicode code point of a dash or space.

Both pairs depict an urban gap or urban spaces. The sound connection varies in strength and is assessed below rather than treated as an automatic compliance failure. I do not infer whether a particular skill or production process was used from appearance.

These scores describe visible decisions, not authorship. Color, maximalism, minimalism, texture, and luminous effects are not disqualifying in themselves.

## C1

The vertical concrete joint is an effective structural decision: it divides “틈의” and “소리,” divides the footer into two usable columns, and gives the largely empty center a purpose. The two sides of the title make the gap part of reading, rather than a decorative background. The restrained field suggests pausing and listening. The concrete has credible scale and varied surface detail, and the serif title gives this image an exhibition character distinct from the other three.

The limitations are also visible. The subtitle crosses the joint and leaves “전시” on its right, so the gap interrupts a secondary reading line less gracefully than the title. The very bold date on the left outweighs the venue column on the right; the footer is competent but not as quiet or balanced as the rest of the page. The sound theme remains implicit: absent the title and subtitle, this could be a strong architecture or material-study poster. The clean black lettering does not substantially engage with the roughness of the concrete. Those choices prevent an exceptional score, though the image has the strongest typographic use of the gap in this group.

## C2

The blue masses and yellow passages produce a clear, memorable silhouette. The diagonals give the large title a natural central chamber and leave a workable lower-left information area. At thumbnail size the title, venue, and date remain easy to locate. The repeated slots can be read as vents, urban openings, or rhythmic marks, so this does connect to the brief without needing a literal waveform.

The connection is comparatively generic. The repeated bars do little beyond establish a beat, and the building-like slabs do not develop a more particular idea of found sound. Large heavy sans type plus uniformly grainy, saturated planes is a familiar music-poster treatment. The type is clean, but the surrounding architecture does more compositional work than the lettering. The overall affect is assertive and energetic; the “small sounds” or attentive-listening dimension is less developed. The saturated palette is not itself a defect. The weakness is the limited specificity of its visual argument and the evenly applied surface treatment. It is the weakest image in the group on the brief's conceptual/editorial aims, despite competent layout.

## D1

This is the strongest finished poster overall. Blocks read as an urban plan, while the routed parallel lines give movement through its interstices. The title occupies a large dark block within that system; it does not merely sit over a separate illustration. The cream information panel at the lower left gives dense artwork a useful place to settle, and the small top band accommodates the subtitle. Orange, navy, cream, and the narrower aqua path have distinct jobs. The title and major date survive reduction especially well.

It is not exceptionally resolved. The line bundles are more like transit or circuit routes than a distinctive acoustic notation, so the “sound” reading is still partly supplied by the words. Some turns and interruptions feel decorative rather than necessary to a specific city/sound relationship. Distress and halftone across many blocks give a coherent print character, but also invoke a familiar retro poster vocabulary. The large date has enough weight to act as a second headline, and the busy neighborhood around the title leaves less contemplative space than C1 or D2. None of these is a blocker; they limit the originality and editorial precision of an otherwise well-controlled image.

## D2

The right-side fissure and left-side text column create a convincing quiet composition. The delicate signal running through the joint makes the sound connection explicit, and the circles place a small listening focus near the middle. The title, subtitle, and lower information block are legible and well separated. At reduced size this remains a calm exhibition poster, rather than dissolving into texture.

Its strongest image idea is also its most conventional: concrete crack plus a luminous signal and radiating rings is readily understandable visual shorthand. The gold line and circles have a different material character from the concrete and clean text; that can be intentional, but the interaction is not developed enough to feel unusually specific. At thumbnail size much of the signal is very slight, leaving the crack to carry most of the image. The title's plain bold sans treatment is serviceable rather than particularly integrated with that crack. The material contrast, generic signal motif, and straightforward typesetting keep execution and anti-slop avoidance at 3. There is no conspicuous malformed object or text artifact.

## Pair diversity and comparative judgment

**Set C has substantial visual diversity.** C1 is quiet, photographic, serif-led, and divided by a literal joint; C2 is chromatic, geometric, sans-led, and driven by angled passages. This is more than a palette change and fulfills the aim of independent alternatives. Each offers a distinct viewing pace and interpretation of urban gaps. C2's comparatively generic rhythm/vent motif and even surface treatment remain weaknesses of that image, rather than weaknesses of consistency between the two.

**Set D also has substantial diversity.** D1 maps movement through urban interstices; D2 concentrates on one fissure and one delicate signal. Density, scale, material, and viewing pace change. The retro ink language of D1 and photographic gold treatment of D2 are valid alternative approaches. I see no pair-diversity failure in either set, and do not award D credit merely because some of its typography is similar.

**Preference: set D, by a modest margin.** With shared campaign identity removed from consideration, D retains a small advantage because both alternatives make the urban-space/sound connection legible, and D1 is the most complete combination of image, type, information, and material. C1 is a credible individual rival and more elegant in its typographic use of the gap than D2. D2 gains clarity from its signal but loses specificity through familiar luminous-line/ripple shorthand; it is not an unqualified improvement on C1. C2 has strong spatial organization and readable information, but its repeated vent bars, broadly familiar music-poster type treatment, and uniformly applied grain develop the brief less specifically than D1. Those observed individual weaknesses support the preference without a family-coherence criterion. Both sets provide meaningfully different alternatives. The margin is modest, the scores are unchanged, and I do not sum them.

**Strongest overall: D1. Weakest: C2.** Confidence is moderate. All four are usable design proposals; none is so unusually specific or fully resolved that remaining defects should be overlooked. This judgment concerns the supplied raster finals at the observed sizes, not an unobserved printed installation.
