## Scope and method

Read only the supplied `brief.md` and inspected A1, A2, B1, and B2 from `blind-review`. Each image was viewed at its original 1024 × 1536 resolution and as a 200 × 300 thumbnail. No maker prompts, reports, revisions, mappings, or other reviews were consulted. The thumbnail files were temporary inspection derivatives; the source images were not changed. This review makes no inference about authorship or production method.

## A1

**Content fit.** The cream corridors between navy and orange blocks read as urban gaps. Parallel orange/navy lines and the broad pale-turquoise path give those corridors a sound-flow metaphor. It has enough energy for independent music while retaining an abstract, exhibition-suitable image. The metaphor is a fairly familiar map-plus-wave solution, but its city and sound relationship is visible rather than dependent on explanatory text.

**Hierarchy and typography.** The huge cream title on a navy area is unmistakably first. The subtitle occupies a quiet cream strip at the top, and the lower-left information wedge holds an unusually assertive date and venue. All required copy reads cleanly at original size. At thumbnail size the title and date remain unusually strong; the subtitle and remaining logistics become small. The bold date nearly competes with the title in weight, and almost every information line is bold, leaving fewer distinctions among secondary levels.

**Composition.** A central title, lower-left information wedge, and winding routes produce an active yet workable reading path. The title has excellent contrast, but the map and lines surround nearly every edge, so the poster has little visual rest. This density is an aesthetic choice, not a constraint failure; it slightly weakens the sense of a quiet, discovered city sound.

**Craft and slop avoidance.** The limited navy/orange/cream palette with one turquoise route is deliberate. Block edges, halftone fills, and visible ink speckle broadly belong to one print vocabulary; the title is integrated into that vocabulary rather than appearing as a detached digital label. The concern is cumulative texture: repeated halftone patches plus distress across most surfaces can read as a familiar retro-poster treatment. There are no salient malformed text, incoherent materials, or meaningless detail cues. Score 4 rather than 5 because the effects are somewhat habitual, not because this aesthetic is disallowed.

## A2

**Content fit.** A physical crack running vertically through concrete is the most direct and convincing “city gap” image here. The fine gold trace contained in the darkness suggests a small, discovered signal. The rings near the middle add a sound cue. The restrained scale of the signal suits sound art and close listening particularly well.

**Hierarchy and typography.** The upper-left bold title and smaller subtitle are immediately intelligible. The bottom-left date, hours, venue, address, admission, and organizer form a clear aligned block with sensible weight differences. The quiet concrete between the two text blocks provides breathing space. At thumbnail size the title survives very well, while the date and venue remain recognizable as distinct information groups. The small address and organizer are necessarily less accessible there, but they are comfortable at original size.

**Composition.** The left text field and right crack have a specific relationship: the type stays in the broad light slab while the crack provides the vertical counterweight. The large vacant center makes the poster feel contemplative. The rings create a focus around mid-height without competing strongly with the headline. The typography uses conventional sans-serif choices rather than especially distinctive lettering, but its placement is purposeful.

**Craft and slop avoidance.** The light left slab, darker right slab, cavities, edge chips, and depth of the crevice form a coherent material scene. The black type remains readable despite the grain. The gold line generally stays within the crack. The rings float visibly over the surface and the luminous trace offers a familiar “hidden energy” effect, so the sound overlay is less materially resolved than the concrete. It is a local, minor artificial cue, not a breakdown of the image. No fake microcopy or malformed Hangul appears. Score 4.

## B1

**Content fit.** The central concrete gap plainly answers the city-crevice theme. Thin warm-orange paths meet at the crack and spread across both slabs, making sound propagation legible at original size. The image is coherent and contemplative enough for a sound-art exhibition, though the symmetrical presentation is closer to a conventional event flyer than a strongly authored gallery poster.

**Hierarchy and typography.** The title is clear at the top. The subtitle is separated from it and the image begins below. The entire logistics block is centered in the bottom cream panel: date, hours, venue, address, admission, and organizer progressively occupy a narrow vertical stack. At thumbnail size this footer is markedly weaker than the title and image; the address, admission, and organizer compress into tiny marks. Original-size text is correct and readable, but the design underuses its 1024 × 1536 area for useful event information. The centered alignment is valid, yet repeated centering and modest weight contrast give the footer little internal structure.

**Composition.** Three rectangular bands—headline, large photo, footer—make the layout orderly but predictable. The central gap is aligned with the centered typography, reinforcing symmetry. The long, blurred foreground on the left gives credible depth but occupies a substantial part of the image without adding much distinct sound information. At thumbnail size the orange curves largely disappear, leaving the picture closer to a generic concrete-gap photograph.

**Craft and slop avoidance.** The concrete texture, focused crack edges, left foreground blur, and darker right slab are materially coherent. The orange curves form smooth decorative arcs across the photo; several spread or end without a visible relation to a particular seam or surface feature. Their softness and the conventional photo-between-text-bands layout are the main generic cues. These are designed-looking choices, not proof of generated defects. Text is clean and there is no meaningless microcopy. Score 3: technically coherent with familiar stock-effect and flyer-layout habits.

## B2

**Content fit.** Blue city-like fragments create cream gaps, and red paths thread through them before flowing toward the title. This gives the urban sound theme a clear graphic representation. As in A1, the metaphor is familiar, but the sound path and empty city space are tightly linked in the visible composition.

**Hierarchy and typography.** The large blue title is the clear anchor in the lower-left region. A bold subtitle sits immediately below, followed by a compact left-aligned logistics block. All copy is clean at original size, and the title, subtitle, and date remain organized at thumbnail size. The subtitle is relatively heavy and close to the title, while the logistics have limited weight differentiation; a little more separation could clarify the hierarchy. The organizer is low but has an adequate bottom margin.

**Composition.** This is the most controlled graphic arrangement of the four. The map occupies the top and right edges, leaving a substantial cream opening on the left. The red sweep bridges that opening and lands just above the title, giving the eye a specific path instead of filling every area. The asymmetric balance and lower title position are concrete editorial decisions. The map stays out of the information block, and the empty field makes both image and text stronger.

**Craft and slop avoidance.** The blue title and blue map share a distressed ink treatment, giving them a common material voice. Small edge breaks and holes have a plausible print relationship rather than a general-purpose photographic texture. The red strokes vary between curves, bars, and dotted portions; those changes make the signal lively but are not consistently explained by the map, so some transitions feel decorative. No incoherent lighting or malformed type is present. Score 4 rather than 5 because distressed-map styling and waveform paths remain familiar cues, with a few arbitrary path transitions.

## Pair assessment and preference

Both pairs deliver a colorful graphic interpretation and a restrained concrete interpretation. That range is useful and within the brief; a shared palette or tightly matched family was not required. Both pairs can plausibly serve the same fictional exhibition through their recurring gap-and-sound subject even though their alignments and palettes differ.

**Preference: A, with moderate rather than decisive confidence.** A1 maintains stronger event-information presence, while A2 gives the small-sound theme a convincing, focused physical image and a spacious text-to-image relationship. B2 is the strongest composition in isolation and could reasonably win against A1 on restraint and editorial balance. B1 pulls B down because the tiny centered logistics, conventional banded arrangement, and faint decorative curves are visibly less persuasive at poster and thumbnail reading scales. A's advantage is therefore the more consistently strong pair, not a universal advantage of its aesthetic.

**Weakest image: B1. Supported repair:** keep the coherent concrete image and title, enlarge the footer information, and replace the long undifferentiated centered stack with two clearly spaced information groups—for example date/hours first and venue/address/admission/organizer second. Give the orange signal fewer, more visible curves anchored to the crevice so it survives the thumbnail; do not increase the overall number of effects. This directly addresses the observed small footer, weak grouping, and disappearing sound cue without requiring a new concept. Recheck the same eight copy lines and 2:3 ratio after the repair.

The closest judgment is A1 versus B2: A1 wins on immediate event information, B2 on spatial discipline. Neither fails the brief, and their different strengths should remain visible in any overall conclusion.
