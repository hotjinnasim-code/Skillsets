# Published poster observations for whole-system direction planning

These are design research observations, not supplied client artwork or mandatory palettes/layouts. Downloaded works remain outside the repository. The coordinator must actually inspect its chosen 3–5 works and adapt principles to the new literal request, not clone imagery or artist names.

- Sulki & Min / Professionalism: red-and-black distributed fighter/wrestler-like cutouts paired with bilingual labels organize the upper field. A very large ON POSTERS title anchors the bottom, flanked by angled date/address groups. Distribution, label rhythm, emphatic title, color and alignment work together; neither the title size nor the image field alone is the solution. Do not certify the depicted participants' identity from pixels or copy the layout as an event template.
- Studio Dumbar / Amsterdam Sinfonietta, as-26 image pair: one poster has a saturated blurred oversized form with a title band and distinct practical-information groups; the adjacent poster uses an ornamental/warped surrounding image. Strong color/digital form can work with a specific full organization. Do not replicate this into five oversized-blob variants.
- Experimental Jetset / SMCS, small outdoor image: a huge date number and opposing red/blue directions govern the page, with the programme comparatively small. Low image resolution limits fine-text inspection. The project relates date/calendar structure to an institution; do not adopt deleted glyphs or shrink this client's practical copy.
- Version Industries / Wells Watson open studio: artist-provided studio photograph reveals a folded canvas/frame boundary, and event details sit within the revealed region. Physical condition, crop, restrained image color, surface and type location support one content-specific scene. Do not invent a photographed provenance for AI-generated output or add paper grit as a substitute for that relationship.
- Pentagram / Dancing on Her Knees: red field, cropped yellow leg and black heel carry a diagonal movement; side credits and slanted lower title participate in its angles. Whole color/crop/layout relationship matters. Do not transfer body crop, stage logo or copy to the neighborhood event.
- Studio Feixen / Grillfest: strong flat primary fields, drawn hands and a diagonal food skewer structure organize dispersed letters and information. The large forms have a party/action relationship; not every object needs to be a shiny 3D blob. Some displaced Latin typography would be inappropriate for exact readable Korean copy.
- Canva event listing, actually root-viewed in public Chrome: visible templates range from an illustrated autumn border surrounding practical event details to photography/layout for concerts, playful birthday graphics and sparse wedding signs. Their audience-recognition and information grouping are useful; listing popularity does not prove high-end finish.
- Canva / Beige and Red Live Music Poster, actually root-viewed as full public print-preview: a rectangular band photo is intersected by the large title at its top and performer name at its bottom; date and venue have a separate aligned grouping and a small boxed invitation. Limited red/cream/black connects image and information. Its stock photo/copy/layout must not be copied.

No source's authorship or anti-AI policy is certified. Observations above distinguish visible construction from interpretation. Sources and exact viewable file pins are in coordinator-reference-pins.json. No research image is a generation attachment, client asset or protected-layout condition.
