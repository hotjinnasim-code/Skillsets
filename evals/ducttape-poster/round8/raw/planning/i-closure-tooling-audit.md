# Candidate I closure tooling: scoped static audit

This is a static source review, not a closed-run integration result or a quality PASS. At assignment, the fifth maker was still running and no I critics had launched. No generated pixels, production records, critic reports/scores, H results, credentials, or unrelated projects were inspected. No actor, image tool, fixture, closure, archive, or replay was run. Only this note was written.

Reviewed the four new ROOT helpers, `planning/candidate-i-pre-generation-freeze.json`, `planning/single-five-case.json`, and the relevant frozen schemas in `evidence_io.py`, `summarize.py`, `audit_source_assets.py`, and `reuse_binding.py`. The runner and review-preparation source were read only to establish their completion, terminal, readiness, and reuse contracts.

The frozen scope is one case, five distinct roles, at most three native attempts per role / fifteen total, and at most six fresh critic sessions. Honest bounded unavailable makers and unready review phases must remain closable as NA with all five roles retained. Fix only the new ROOT helpers; do not alter frozen inputs, actor evidence, scores, or thresholds.

## Necessary fixes

1. **Complete maker inventory and runner/caller binding are not established at closure.** `seal_single_five.py:36–59` trusts completion booleans/session IDs and the terminal's listed stages. It neither compares that list to the complete real `stages/` inventory nor checks the runner's terminal-report hash, launch fields, trace pin/events, or actual fresh argv. It checks a caller's role/stage and an allowed outcome but does not join its outcome or explicitly returned source identifiers to the corresponding manifest/failure. Consequently an omitted failed stage undercounts calls; a terminal changed after runner completion can be sealed; and cache equality alone does not establish the caller/source join. The frozen contracts already require contiguous `initial`, `repair-1`, `repair-2` stages, exact outcome/artifact/input/selection bindings, and all-three-failed unavailable terminals (`evidence_io.py:82–103,197–279`; `prepare_reviews_v2.py:89–177`). The runner pins its terminal output and trace (`run_agent.py:11–22`). Revalidate those complete contracts in the ROOT wrapper, require caller/outcome/source agreement for each real attempted stage, verify maker completion/report/trace joins, and derive counts from that inventory. Recheck readiness's pinned terminal inventory against these current terminals. Assert the exact five role IDs and disjoint actual maker/critic session identities, rather than relying only on cardinalities at `seal_single_five.py:62,91`.

2. **Honest unavailable / unready outcomes cannot currently be closed.** `seal_single_five.py:67–75` requires both waves to be READY, requires summaries, and requires source-attestation PASS. A genuine initial failure followed by a successful repair creates an UNREADY initial wave; a role with all three actual failures creates an honest unavailable member (`prepare_reviews_v2.py:321–345`). Both are rejected instead of being retained as NA. An existing raw review with a source-attestation NA is likewise rejected. Preserve the pinned readiness/failure records, all five member identities and actual call/session counts, represent an unready phase explicitly as NA, and replay only summaries that actually exist. Preserve the original v4 gates separately; source-attestation NA must not become a quality PASS. Do not fabricate missing scores or launch replacement critics. A zero-summary replay must not be presented as five-image quality certification (`verify_archive.py:46,71`).

3. **Raw invalid observations are mistaken for authoritative pins, breaking NA preservation.** `archive_single_five.py:10–16,38–44` recursively validates every nested `path` + SHA-256 pair. Frozen summaries retain raw critic `viewed` and transcription `revisit` claims inside `raw_data` / `raw` (`summarize.py:333–355,492–506,551`). A wrong observed hash or nonexistent observed path correctly produces NA, but this traversal rejects the archive instead of preserving that raw record. `verify_archive.py:30–38` similarly rejects every unmapped decoded `path`, so an erroneous raw observation cannot reach the frozen validator to reproduce its NA outcome. Traverse only schema-defined authoritative packet/manifest/runner/reuse pins; keep raw claims as exact bytes. Make the in-memory resolver distinguish mapped authoritative artifacts from literal invalid observations, and prevent fallback reads from original host paths. Unknown observation identifiers must remain invalid during replay without being treated as missing required archive artifacts. Cover both wrong hashes and unmapped paths during later integration.

4. **Portable replay does not bind its conclusion to final-evidence gates/counts or all frozen pin claims.** `verify_archive.py:25–27` compares the two copies of final evidence and checks only a few cardinalities. At `44–69`, replay is compared to `summary.json`, but not to the final item's `gate`, `original_v4_set_gate`, `original_v4_image_gates`, stage-wide gate maps/counts, source-attestation claim, or aggregate actual-review counts/sessions. An inconsistent final-evidence PASS can therefore survive while the unchanged stored summary correctly replays FAIL/NA. Validate the exact case/role/wave inventory from the pinned corpus and compare every emitted final gate/count/session field to the unchanged summaries, readiness and runner records, counting a reused summary only once. Resolve and byte-check authoritative final/freeze pin claims through the archive index before importing the replay modules; an index row digest by itself does not verify agreement with the pre-generation helper pins. Keep replay-integrity PASS distinct from the preserved quality gates.

5. **Source-side path checks and gallery artifact binding need completion.** `archive_single_five.py:25–28` checks symlinks only after skipping non-files, so symlink directories are not rejected; `40–44` resolves external pins before checking their lexical symlink chain. `seal_single_five.py:16,47` also resolves pins and accepts a terminal-provided stage path without first enforcing the frozen stage-name/real-directory contract. Reject symlink entries/ancestors in owned evidence paths before resolution and require owned case/role/stage/packet artifacts to remain under their expected ROOT directories. Continue to allow explicitly authoritative external native sources, with a declared-identifier-to-archived-file mapping that covers the exact path identifiers consumed by replay. The archive-side containment/symlink check at `verify_archive.py:17–20` is useful but occurs after collection. Separately, `build_single_five_gallery.py:14–15` uses lexical `relative_to(root)` and trusts selected-file metadata without checking existence, hashes, or selected-manifest binding; a `ROOT/../...` identifier becomes an escaping link, and missing/mismatched images still increment the “actual selected” count. Validate those selected artifacts and resolved containment before rendering/counting them; retain missing roles in the denominator and show unavailable records honestly. Preserve the `raw/production/...` layout so the gallery's relative links remain valid in an archive.

## Static checks that did not identify a required rule change

`reuse_binding.py:7–25` compares full evaluation context, preserves the original complete three critics and their exact report/launch/completion/trace pins, requires fifteen image/profile records and 105 valid integer score values for this five-image case, and does not require high scores or quality PASS. Thus low scores and well-formed mandatory NA gates remain eligible for unchanged whole-case reuse; invalid/incomplete procedure records are correctly ineligible. `seal_single_five.py:86–89` counts a reused initial summary only once. Keep those semantics.

The archiver copies source files byte-for-byte, retains the raw tree including unselected stages/failures, and indexes external authoritative sources. The verifier hashes mapped archive files and rejects archive-relative traversal and archive symlink escapes. These are useful mechanisms, but they do not resolve the binding and NA-preservation gaps above.

Root must perform the actual closed-run integration after production/reviews finish and after the helper fixes, including portable replay without original host paths and gallery link checks. This review makes no completion or quality claim for the unfinished run.
