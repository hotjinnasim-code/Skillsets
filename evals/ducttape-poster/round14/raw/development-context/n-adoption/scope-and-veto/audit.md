**Significant problems and minimal recommendations**

1. **The exclusion predicate drifts between stages, risking both loopholes and excessive rejection.**

   N art direction is the clearest definition: “고딕 제목·안내 블록이 화면을 지배하는 구성,” with both single and divided colour fields judged by their actual overall impression. Its cosmetic-addition clause is conditional: the large background/text-block relationship must remain unchanged. [N art direction:53](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/art-direction.md:53). The leading planning sentence in N SKILL:36 and the delegated planning rule in N parallel production:9 describe flat fields with Gothic title/information blocks without expressly repeating dominance. Conversely, core QA names a Gothic **title**, while central QA abbreviates the field to “단색 바탕,” omitting near-solid and split-field wording. [N SKILL:36](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/SKILL.md:36); [N parallel production:9](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/parallel-production.md:9); [N SKILL:64](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/SKILL.md:64); [N parallel production:33](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/parallel-production.md:33).

   The document-wide reading closes split-colour, faint-texture, tiny-image, and bottom-strip evasions. A literal reading of the shorter QA clauses can reopen them. A returned image with a serif title, unchanged Gothic information panels, and the same flat-field template also challenges the “Gothic title” trigger even though planning expressly rejects a title-only font swap. Changing every text block's font is not expressly addressed by the title-only clause; the broader central instruction against “단순 폰트 교체” helps, but the detection wording still depends on Gothic blocks. These are hypothetical execution paths, not observed outputs.

   The opposite risk is treating any flat negative space plus a Gothic title and readable information as prohibited, even where substantive imagery and spatial organization define a different whole design. The permission for Gothic information type does not explicitly mention Gothic titles. Meanwhile, large titles and title-first reading remain permitted elsewhere. [N SKILL, lines 30 and 36](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/SKILL.md:30); [N art direction:17](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/art-direction.md:17).

   **Minimal recommendation:** define the excluded whole composition once, and have planning, native inputs, maker QA, and central QA use that same definition. The decisive relationship should be a passive solid/near-solid or divided colour field organized predominantly by the rejected title/information-block template, with imagery remaining subordinate. Cosmetic additions or font-only substitutions do not cure that unchanged organization. State that Gothic titles **and** information type are permitted in other compositions. Do not introduce colour-count rules, fixed image-area percentages, a mandatory giant object, or a ban on all typography-led designs.

2. **The override exception is explicit in planning but implicit in local QA and imagery requirements.**

   N SKILL:36 says “사용자가 이후 해당 구성을 명시적으로 요구하면 그 요청을 우선,” and rightly says ordinary “미니멀·모던·깔끔” wording does not lift the exclusion. N art direction:53 strengthens this to “나중에 … 경우만.” Delegated planning and the evaluation condition also recognize a later explicit whole-composition request. [N SKILL:36](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/SKILL.md:36); [N art direction:53](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/art-direction.md:53); [N parallel production:9](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/parallel-production.md:9); [N evaluation loop:31](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/evaluation-loop.md:31).

   Core QA nevertheless says to record the visual configuration as a violation, and central QA says “통과시키지 않는다,” without checking whether this exclusion remains active for that image. Research likewise applies the default exclusion without a local exception. A whole-document interpretation can inherit the exception, but an isolated maker/QA instruction can overrule a deliberately authorized later result. The “later only” wording can also make a fresh request that explicitly specifies this composition appear weaker than an identical follow-up, despite the reusable skill's general priority for user style conditions. [N SKILL:64](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/SKILL.md:64); [N parallel production:33](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/parallel-production.md:33); [N visual research:9](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/visual-research.md:9); [N SKILL:20](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/SKILL.md:20).

   There is a related exception mismatch: the new art-direction paragraph requires “비문자 시각 체계가 내용과 인상을 실질적으로 맡게,” whereas both the main skill and art-direction QA expressly prioritize a user's typography-only/no-image request. [N art direction:55](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/art-direction.md:55); [N SKILL:38](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/SKILL.md:38); [N art direction:79](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/art-direction.md:79).

   **Minimal recommendation:** resolve the latest explicit applicable user instructions before each phase, and label the exclusion as active or explicitly overridden for the relevant image/request. Carry that status into research, native specification, QA, and final selection. An explicit whole-composition override should not require a second ritual confirmation or a particular sequence of turns. A typography-only request overrides the nontext requirement; it does **not**, by itself, authorize the separately excluded flat-field/Gothic template. A request for a Gothic font, a photograph, or generic minimalism also does not waive the whole-style exclusion. Preserve the existing one-image scope for follow-up changes.

3. **Universal native-specification propagation is less explicit than delegated propagation.**

   The only new direct instruction to put the exclusion into the actual production specification is N parallel production:9. The main skill reads that reference when collaboration tools exist; its direct-production fallback promises the same quantity, directions, and QA criteria. The universal native-input paragraph specifies copy once, necessary user conditions, selected relationships, finish, and output conditions, but does not expressly name the active default exclusion. [N parallel production:9](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/parallel-production.md:9); [N SKILL, lines 44–48](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/SKILL.md:44). The maker's shorter native-input paragraph also relies on “필요한 조건.” [N parallel production:21](/Users/nahyeonbin/Desktop/_Codex/ducttape-poster-n-generalization-review/draft-n/references/parallel-production.md:21).

   This is a propagation ambiguity, not proof that delegated specifications omit the veto. On a direct run, or a concise local-edit specification, the runtime can communicate a positive layout while leaving the exclusion only in its planning/QA context. That falls short of an equally explicit instruction at all three stages requested in the change record and can spend capped corrections recovering a default template that the native input never expressly ruled out.

   **Minimal recommendation:** add one sentence to the core native-input rule requiring every initial generation and edit specification, on both delegated and direct paths, to carry the active whole-style exclusion or the scoped explicit user override. Keep the positive image/space/type relationship concrete. Preserve copy once; do not paste the complete request, planning JSON, or critic rubric into the image call.
