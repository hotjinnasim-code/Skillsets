**Reusable principle:** one shared movement can travel through several content zones; shape, type, and background can collaborate instead of occupying separate decorative slots. **Adaptation limit:** this degree of cropped type would obscure Korean workshop names. Preserve a fully readable title and two action names; let only supporting shapes or repeats cross boundaries. Do not copy the distinctive ribbons, words, palette, or grid arrangement.

## Transfer to the reusable workflow

| Reference | Non-text contribution and transfer limit |
|---|---|
| Feixen | Curves/grids contribute motion, continuity, and surface rhythm; they do **not** establish the intended making/reading actions. This is a composition reference, not permission for a pure typography concept. |
| SABF | Small photographic fragments insert book/place specificity into a repeated field; new original action imagery must replace city landmarks and its lettering. |
| Mut | Color/image treatment supplies atmosphere under a stable hierarchy; the red field alone does not show what participants do. A generated concept needs visual action evidence beyond its words. |
| Canva | Two finished sheets concretely suggest art-making; add a visible sharing/reading relationship, rather than simply adding a reading label. |

Use references to choose a **whole relationship among content, shape, image, type, color, surface, crop, and emotional tone** before generation. These examples support three substantially different mechanisms: continuous overlapping field; modular civic collage; shared adult-program hierarchy with variable image treatment. Canva supplies an accessible fourth mechanism: overlapping made sheets with hand marks. They should not collapse into five versions of large headline + single giant object.

For the intended printmaking + public reading event, the deciding semantic question is whether the visual shows **a trace being made and then shared/read**. Two icon stickers merely coexisting do not show that relationship. Reference principles can inform an original sequence, shared surface, repeated impression, or transition; none of the downloaded marks/images should be transplanted. This is adaptation guidance, not a proposed design or test result.
