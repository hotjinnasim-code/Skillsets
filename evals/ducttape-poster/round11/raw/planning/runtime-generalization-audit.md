## Behavioral risks and retained constraints

- **False unity or invented chronology:** edit 1 explicitly limits relationships to the request and prioritizes user-specified separation, layout, and viewpoint. A combined poster may contain distinct places or moments without inventing one shared physical scene. Do not add people, actions, or sequence to title-only input.
- **Layout convergence:** edit 2 specifies a function and a choice of mechanisms, not columns, cards, arrows, one continuous path, or a fixed fact order. Apply the existing comparison with surface/style names hidden so resolving retrieval does not collapse five directions into one layout.
- **Punctuation overcorrection:** edit 4 must not turn every short mark into an en dash or alter fixed spaces. User-specified font/style/geometry still constrains the available optical adjustments. An unresolved reading remains uncertainty, not a reason to rewrite the copy or weaken the gate.
- **Inspection or prompt expansion:** edit 3 uses the existing thumbnail/use/native review. A maker's short direction can carry the chosen action relationship and next useful information; do not append this audit, a checklist, or repeated copy to native image prompts. Record only the actual inspected display dimensions and remaining issues.
- **User precedence:** quantity, aspect ratio, style, expression, assets, fixed copy, preserved geometry, and explicit type-only requests continue to override general defaults. There is no new nontext requirement beyond the current user-conditional one.
- **True five independent makers:** retain the existing independence and concurrency rules in `references/parallel-production.md`; edit 5 changes only the authority of central design choices. The coordinator designs the whole set; five actual makers each produce one assigned direction, never reading another maker's generated image/prompt or recursively recruiting makers. A lower concurrency cap means queued independent roles, not invented simultaneous execution or one maker producing all five. Preserve independent paths/history and the existing maximum two corrections per image across coordinator and maker. A maker reports why major relationships changed instead of silently replacing its assigned whole system.
- **Evaluation integrity:** retain `references/evaluation-loop.md` unchanged. R10 remains closed; its FAIL and NA cannot be relabeled by K. No fresh critic, image repair, or test was run for this audit. Repeating the known R10 request would be development. The coordinator's prospective R11 uses new, previously unproduced/unreviewed content; a frozen one-request/five-maker run can provide bounded transfer evidence for that content and its constraints. It cannot establish a universal result, stable success rate, or isolated causal effect of K. If R11 feedback is then used to change K or choose new images, those later actions are development and must be recorded separately from the original new-content check.

No edits are proposed to `agents/openai.yaml`, `references/visual-research.md`, or `references/evaluation-loop.md`, or to actor transport. Preserve the interface, maker independence, research, copy/ambiguity procedure, seven-dimension gate, and finite evaluation loop.
