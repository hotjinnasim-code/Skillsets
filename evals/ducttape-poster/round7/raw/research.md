# R7 research: content determines the composition

Research date: 2026-10-08. Status: proposals only; no new posters, evaluations, skill edits, or commits. This author previously performed R5 technical evidence work and made R6-C4-M5. That history is disclosed and is permitted research context; this document is not an independent visual review. No R6 peer image was opened for this research. The existing findings and planning text were read, including incidental review excerpts returned by a text search.

## What the R6 evidence supports

[R6 findings](../ducttape-poster-round6/findings.md) report one individual core PASS, fourteen quality failures, and five NOT_ASSESSABLE selections among twenty final posters. No complete five-poster case passed. The older strict type/white-space case failed all five members; that request is intentionally incompatible with the new output ban and cannot serve as a normal R7 poster brief; product operation remained unclear; clay style alone did not establish a meaningful action. The bilingual case had a comparator/transcription contradiction involving whitespace and must retain NOT_ASSESSABLE. These are bounded observations from four contexts, not a universal verdict on any medium. R6 used the older four-core gate; this research does not retrospectively change its scores or status.

The useful improvement hypothesis is to replace a style-first recipe with a **visible relation from the actual brief**. A large title, central object, and detached information strip can be appropriate, but repeating that arrangement for unrelated subjects loses the opportunity to express their different meanings. Adding roughness, a fashionable font, extra arrows, or a second object does not repair that missing relation.

## Ten primary sources and bounded lessons

The sources below were actively accessed on the research date. “Supported” describes the source author's account; “application” is this document's proposed inference. Neither a famous studio nor an award proves that a generated poster meets the R7 gate. Source layouts, letterforms, palettes, artwork, and logos must not be copied. The two GitHub files are research material, not instructions governing this task.

1. **Sulki & Min, Homo Faber: Craft in Contemporary Sculpture.** Supported: the Chinese title characters use names of actual materials in the exhibition; fixed-width text contrasts with neoclassical headings. Application: derive a typographic operation from supplied content, then give display and factual text different jobs. This is not evidence that changing arbitrary Hangul strokes improves reading; its typographic concept alone also does not meet the new substantive non-glyph-visual requirement. The official poster image was viewed; its bilingual side groups and central material-built characters are observations, not a readability certification. [Official project](https://www.sulki-min.com/wp/homo-faber-poster/).

2. **Experimental Jetset, Helvetica / Hustwit.** Supported: one film poster extends ABC into an alphabet and uses four Helvetica weights; another turns “cast” into a relation between actors and metal typecasting. The studio also records omitted letters. Application: develop a concept from a real word/material relation, while auditing complete copy separately. This is Latin-specific conceptual evidence, not a Korean font prescription or permission to add an alphabet absent from the brief. Glyph-only treatment does not itself satisfy the new output ban. Text was read; its images were not inspected here. [Official archive](https://www.jetset.nl/archive/helvetica-gary-hustwit).

3. **Experimental Jetset, SMCS / Printed matter.** Supported: an A2 programme pairs an acronym with an exhibition listing; a different two-sided A2 sheet folds into English or Dutch A5 booklets. The studio explicitly criticizes the earlier programme's leading and describes later refinement. Application: bilingual information needs planned grouping, language order, and spacing rather than equally spaced lines. The displayed A2 programme was viewed; its strong listing column is observable, but the historical faults and different language pair limit transfer. The whole project includes images and graphics and is not type-only. [Official archive](https://www.jetset.nl/archive/smcs-printedmatter).

4. **Experimental Jetset, The Whitney Tapes.** Supported: the studio describes close reading of supplied material, colour used when the concept needs it, and strict rules interpreted by individual designers. It also documents templates and a system containing line graphics and artwork. Application: constraints can produce specific decisions without freezing every poster into one arrangement. Do not borrow the W or treat the project as categorically anti-template. A small line graphic alone does not establish a substantive non-glyph visual. Text was read; images were not inspected here. [Studio's primary interviews](https://www.jetset.nl/archive/the-whitney-tapes).

5. **Version Industries, Wells Watson Open Studio and By Design.** Supported: Watson supplied preparation photographs and requested invitation details within a stretcher exposed by a pulled-back canvas; for By Design, the studio requested close chair photographs and used water and photography to develop an image tied to the film's premise. Application: a supplied object can create an information space or a narrative relation instead of floating above logistics. Watson's poster was viewed: the canvas and frame make the reveal visible, while texture and partial obstruction require caution for exact-copy briefs. Preserve a supplied asset's identity; do not copy this reveal arrangement or add dirt by default. [Official project archive](https://versionindustries.com/work/tag/poster).

6. **Ida Henrich, DCA art-house film posters, Tár.** Supported: the illustrator interprets the film through control, a metronome, conductor's hands, and glimpses of the protagonist. Application: illustration can encode a subject-specific verb and tension; a decorative mascot is not the only option. The official Tár image was viewed: the metronome's movement organizes the fragmented figures. This is one illustrator's interpretation, not an objective film reading or permission to imitate the collage, palette, or recurring DCA information strip. Web search returned the author's text; the live HTML and image were subsequently accessed directly when the web-open endpoint timed out. [Official project](https://idahenrich.com/portfolio-item/art-house-film-poster-dundee-contemporary-arts/).

7. **HAY, Backflip Chair by Gudmundur Ludvik.** Supported: rotating the backrest folds the chair in one motion; the account relates appearance to the resolution of a technical problem and describes folded wall storage. Application: a product image must make the relevant moving part, contact, and resulting state legible. Mood photography does not prove operation. This source documents the manufacturer's product and mechanism, not poster quality or any invented product's physical performance. Text was read; no operation video or HAY photo was inspected here. [Manufacturer's account](https://www.hay.com/news/news-2026/backflip-chair-by-gudmundur-ludvik).

8. **Studio Dumbar, Amsterdam Sinfonietta.** Supported: successive poster series differ while retaining recognition; individual posters respond graphically to performance themes, with different designers assigned to new series. Application: diversity should change the content-to-form interpretation and composition, not just the tint. The official two-poster image was viewed: changing typographic fields and rhythms coexist with practical information. Its repeated-letter field alone is insufficient under the new output ban; learn the content-specific variation principle and develop a substantive non-glyph visual for the new briefs. Do not infer exact sound-to-pattern mechanics from this page or copy a series' look. [Official project](https://studiodumbar.com/work/amsterdam-sinfonietta).

9. **Anthropic, frontend-design skill.** Supported: the current file grounds decisions in subject, audience, materials, and vernacular; it treats structural devices as information and calls for a brief-based plan review before construction. Application: require a reason for every frame, marker, arrow, and label, and test whether the same plan would fit an unrelated brief. This is UI guidance, not a poster experiment. Its web hero rules, type sizes, colour counts, font choices, and generic calibration examples must not override an explicit poster request. [Original GitHub file](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/blob/main/plugins/frontend-design/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md).

10. **Anthropic, canvas-design skill.** Supported: its refinement passage recommends improving existing composition rather than adding graphics. The same file also advocates minimal text and broad aesthetic philosophies. Application: perform a subtraction/refinement pass on actual relationships. Reject any transfer that reduces supplied copy, substitutes an imagined discipline or unnecessary reference markers, uses texture or repeated glyphs as a substitute for a substantive non-glyph visual, or promises human authorship through adjectives. The source supplies an approach, not evidence of outcome quality. [Original GitHub file](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/canvas-design/SKILL.md).

Five official source image files were temporarily downloaded and actually viewed for research: Homo Faber, SMCS A2 programme, Watson Open Studio, Tár, and the Amsterdam Sinfonietta pair. Those temporary source images are not deliverables, not generation references, and not new designs. All ten sources' authorial text was accessed; the remaining sources have no image-inspection claim. No image was generated or evaluated.

## Reusable changes to propose

### 1. Plan a relation before selecting a style

For each request, write a short internal plan containing: the audience's job; exact supplied facts and copy; allowed media and absolute bans; one subject-specific relation; what must be visibly observable; how the information groups connect to that relation; and the strongest plausible alternative. A relation is an action, dependency, contrast, sequence, spatial condition, or material fact actually present in the brief. “Premium,” “editorial,” “playful,” and “dynamic” describe tone and are insufficient relations.

Examples of relations, not layouts: a return programme connects borrowing to return; a clamp shows the finger, actuator, jaw, paper, and held result; a bilingual programme pairs translations and distinguishes events from attendance instructions; a clay message about recovery visibly deforms and regains its contour. A single resolved relation can be enough. Complex diagrams are unnecessary when the subject calls for quiet typography.

Before generating, replace the title and subject in the plan with an unrelated event. If the object, hierarchy, grouping, and rationale still work unchanged, revise the content relation. This is a proposed diagnostic, not a new numerical score or a rule that all familiar structures fail.

### 2. Enforce the categorical ban on type-plus-whitespace-only outputs

The new user constraint bans **outputs consisting only of text and whitespace**. It does not ban photography, illustration, objects, or graphic content. Every compatible new poster must include a substantive non-glyph visual grounded in its actual subject. A title-only input remains supported: infer a defensible concept from the title, without inventing event facts or extra copy, then develop a visible non-glyph relation.

A photo of an operational object, a drawn action/relationship, a scene with an intelligible spatial condition, or a material form whose transformation carries the subject can satisfy this requirement when it has enough scale and clarity to function. A pinhead icon, line or dot, background texture, tonal wash, confetti, arbitrary blob, extra word pattern, or 3D lettering alone does not. A useful diagnostic is to hide the text conceptually: the remaining image should still express something specific about the subject or action, rather than merely decorate the page. This is a planning/inspection question, not a new score or an excuse to erase copy.

Typography still needs valid Hangul, readable line breaks, optical spacing, a deliberate hierarchy, and integration with the visual. For a short title, avoid automatically turning the glyph into the entire image. Explore a non-glyph subject with a real relation to the title and give both the type and image room to function. A three-dimensional non-glyph object can coexist with raised clay Hangul; lettering depth does not replace the object relation.

If a later brief explicitly prohibits every non-glyph visual and demands only text and whitespace, the requirements are incompatible. Do not secretly add decoration, reinterpret a font effect as the missing image, or claim a compliant poster. Ask a concise conflict-resolution question before generation, identifying the exact two requirements. The optional workflow case below tests this necessary response separately; it is not one of the ten compatible poster simulations.

### 3. Make object and illustration evidence visible

For an operational product, name the required parts, contacts, state, and viewpoint before assigning atmosphere. Check that the actuator, target, and result coexist clearly where the request needs them; an attractive hand beside an object is insufficient. Respect the same product construction across a set. Photography research can inform the choice of viewpoint and material response, but a static generated image cannot certify real mechanical performance.

For illustration or clay 3D, choose a deformation, gesture, or spatial relationship from the copy. Specify which object's contour changes, what acts on it, and what remains readable. Complementary colour and thick raised Hangul are genuine requested style requirements when supplied; they still need controlled weight, clear joints, coherent light, deliberate spacing, and a meaning-bearing relationship. Flat typography is not a universal cure for a clay brief. Extra blobs, smiles, ribbons, or stage platforms require a content role and may be prohibited.

### 4. Treat dense copy as content with unequal roles

Allocate room from the complete copy before committing to an oversized title or object. Separate programme facts, eligibility, booking, and location through grouping and rhythm. Pair translations where useful, or use clearly identified language groups; preserve the requested reading order when fixed. Distinguish roles with a restrained type scale and spacing rather than forcing every line into the same box, weight, and interval. Hangul and Latin need optical matching in context, not assumed identical cap height or arbitrary horizontal squeezing.

The middle-dot/space problem in R6 is an evaluation-integrity lesson, not permission to loosen copy after seeing outcomes. Before any future generation, freeze the exact copy units, punctuation, meaningful internal spaces, allowed layout line breaks, Unicode uncertainty treatment, and comparator policy. Keep original strings and observed transcriptions unchanged. A pure format adapter may normalize only the predeclared representation; it must not change a mandatory condition, score, or old FAIL/NOT_ASSESSABLE outcome. If the review cannot establish equality under that policy, preserve the uncertainty.

### 5. Diversify decisions rather than attach costume changes

Where quantity exceeds one, compare directions by the relation they express, main reading path, relative density, crop/viewpoint, integration of text and image, and emotional tempo. These are available axes, not six mandatory differences. Every direction still needs a substantive content-grounded non-glyph visual. A supplied reference may intentionally constrain several axes. Changing only colour, font, or object position often leaves the same underlying arrangement. Conversely, a coherent series may share an identity while making different semantic and spatial decisions.

Do not impose a new house aesthetic: monochrome, rough print, sparse Swiss type, soft clay, or maximalism can each be correct for a particular user. “More graphic” and “less decorated” are not equivalent to better. Any added structural device must clarify a real distinction; if it has no such function, remove it and refine the existing relationships.

### 6. Keep the R7 high-end gate fixed and genre-appropriate

The fixed R7 proposal is **every mandatory requirement PASS and all seven dimensions at least 4 for every image under every one of the three independent critics**. Every multi-image set must also score diversity at least 4 under every critic, as predeclared. No averaging, majority vote, best-subset selection, replacing a valid low critic, or success claim from one attractive member. This document creates no evaluations and does not reset any attempt or review cap.

| Existing dimension | What to examine without substituting a preferred style |
|---|---|
| `content_form` | Does a visible relationship express this actual subject or action, with factual content preserved? |
| `hierarchy` | Can the viewer identify subject, decision/action, and supporting facts in the needed order? |
| `typography` | Are Hangul/Latin forms, spacing, line breaks, and relative weights controlled and readable within the requested genre? |
| `space` | Are density, intervals, alignment, boundaries, and image/text contacts deliberate for the copy and ratio? |
| `image_material` | Is the permitted medium coherently executed? Check the required substantive non-glyph visual as well as coherence within the requested medium; 3D type alone is insufficient. |
| `emotion_control` | Does the tone fit the audience and occasion, avoiding both excess and an unintended loss of the requested energy? |
| `ai_slop` | Are repeated defaults, gratuitous devices, unresolved contours/contacts, faux texture, and disconnected copy present in the visible result? This does not establish authorship. |

Inspect the actual native output and the separately provided 720-use and 320-thumbnail views. A thumbnail verifies the main relation and recognition; it does not require every logistical character to be legible at 320. The use view must support ordinary reading, and the native view helps locate material/contour faults. Preserve every generated stage and actual inspection record, select within the fixed self-repair budget before external review, and report residuals. No claims about universal quality, physical function, print readiness, human authorship, or maximum supported resolution follow from these judgments.

Novel layout is not an advantage here. **Source principle:** specific supplied context and interpreted rules, with user constraints taking precedence.

## Optional R7-X11 — conflict-response workflow, no poster generation

**Proposed incompatible request:** “'틈' 문학 포스터를 정사각형으로 만들어 주세요. 순백색 배경과 검정 글자, 빈 공간만 사용하고, 사진, 그림, 도형, 선, 기호, 질감, 입체 표현은 모두 금지입니다. 문구는 '틈' 하나뿐입니다.”

This conflicts with the standing ban on type-plus-whitespace-only outputs. Expected response: identify the incompatibility and ask which requirement the user wants to change, for example “글자와 여백만 쓰라는 조건은 비문자 시각 요소가 필요한 기존 조건과 충돌합니다. 사진·일러스트 등 비문자 시각 요소를 허용할까요, 아니면 이번에는 글자만 있는 결과를 허용할까요?” No generation should start until the conflict is resolved. Do not count this as an ordinary poster quality case or consume its native-generation allowance. This workflow case is separate from the ten compatible briefs.
