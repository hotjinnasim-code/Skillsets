# Blind art-direction review — round 1

## Method and limits

I reviewed only `blind-review/A1.png`, `A2.png`, `B1.png`, `B2.png`, the shared `brief.md`, and my prewritten anchored design rubric. I did not read version folders, prompts, maker reports, diffs or other conclusions. A1/A2 constitute set A; B1/B2 constitute set B. These labels do not identify a skill version.

Every original was viewed at its native 1024 × 1536 size, then again as a 256 × 384 review derivative. All have the requested 2:3 portrait ratio. Full-size inspection supports typography/craft judgments; reduced inspection supports hierarchy. Physical exhibition print size, distance, color-managed proofing and actual print resolution were not specified and cannot be verified from these files.

Ratings use separate 1–5 dimensions. 3 means competent/usable; 5 requires exceptional evidence. 2 and 4 are intermediate. There is no sum score. The added seventh dimension assesses visible generic/synthetic-looking cues and art-direction specificity; it is not an authorship detector and does not prohibit any style.

The sample is one brief with two images per condition, so broader success-rate claims are unsupported.

## Largest supported weakness and repair target

**B1 has the largest visible weakness: typography and the sound gesture do not share the fissure's spatial logic.** The result combines an interchangeable header/photo/footer template with a gold-strand knot. This is a meaningful art-direction problem even though its copy and photographic craft are good.

If B1 is being improved, recompose its title/subtitle and information so they relate to one concrete plane and to the narrow gap, and replace the many drifting strands with a small, explicitly rooted sound gesture whose location, scale and trajectory have a reason. Preserve all exact copy, the concrete subject and the quiet emotional register. This warrants composition-level re-art-direction rather than polishing grain or adding decoration.

B2 has a smaller local opportunity: distinguish date and venue from address/host through scale/spacing, while preserving its already effective mass/void composition. I do not recommend a blanket prohibition on gold, grain, retro printing, centered type, photographic cracks, or dense maps. The supported lesson is to require a visible relationship between the subject, major spatial/type decisions and decorative effects; a style blacklist would overfit this pilot.
